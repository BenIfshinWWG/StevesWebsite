If you need a lawyer

If you are a naturalized citizen facing denaturalization and you want the Clearinghouse to try to find you a volunteer lawyer, please use the citizen intake form rather than email, so we can collect the details we need. If you are a lawyer who wants to volunteer, please use the lawyer sign-up form.

Please do not send sensitive documents or urgent deadline questions by email expecting an immediate response. The Clearinghouse cannot guarantee a timely reply, does not provide legal advice, and is not your lawyer. If you have an approaching court deadline, seek legal help immediately through any means available to you.
