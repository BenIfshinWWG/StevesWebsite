Volunteer to defend citizens facing denaturalization

The Clearinghouse is building a network of lawyers willing to represent naturalized U.S. citizens in civil denaturalization cases on a pro bono (free of charge) basis.

Two ways to volunteer

We welcome two kinds of volunteers:

  • Experienced counsel. Lawyers who have defended civil denaturalization cases (cases the government brings in federal court to revoke citizenship) or who have substantial federal civil litigation experience.
  • Lawyers willing to be trained. Lawyers who do not have denaturalization experience but are willing to receive training and, where appropriate, serve alongside a more experienced co-counsel or mentor.

What pro bono representation through the Clearinghouse involves

  • Representing a matched citizen in a civil denaturalization proceeding in federal court.
  • For those who need it, training and/or mentorship support may be available to help you take on a matter.
  • Any representation is arranged directly between you and the citizen under your own engagement agreement. The Clearinghouse is not a party to that relationship and does not supervise the representation.

What we can and cannot promise

The Clearinghouse will attempt to match volunteers with citizens who need help, but it does not guarantee that any particular lawyer will be referred a case, or that a match will be found. Signing up does not obligate you to accept any matter, and it does not obligate the Clearinghouse to send you one.
