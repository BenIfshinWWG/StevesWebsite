What happens next

  • The Clearinghouse will attempt to identify a volunteer lawyer who may be willing to consider your case on a pro bono basis.
  • A response is not guaranteed, and finding a lawyer is not guaranteed.
  • No attorney-client relationship has been formed with the Clearinghouse or with any lawyer by submitting this form.
  • You should receive a confirmation email restating this information. If you do not, please check your spam folder or contact us.
If you have an urgent deadline: If you have a response date in a court case or another approaching deadline, do not wait for the Clearinghouse. Seek legal help immediately through any means available to you. You should also seek help independently if at all possible, rather than relying solely on this Clearinghouse.
