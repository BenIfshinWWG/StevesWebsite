Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Clearinghouse collects through this website, why we collect it, how it is stored and protected, who it may be shared with, how long we keep it, and how you can ask us to delete it.

What we collect

  • From citizens using the intake form: your name, contact information (email, phone, city and state), preferred language and contact method, the status of your matter (a threat or a filed lawsuit), related dates, the court and case number if applicable, any description you provide, and, if you choose to upload it, a copy of your complaint or summons.
  • From lawyers using the sign-up form: your name, firm/organization, contact information, bar admissions and jurisdictions, federal districts, experience, capacity, and preferences.
  • Automatically: limited technical information needed to operate the site securely (for example, information used to prevent spam and abuse). We do not use the site to advertise to you.

Why we collect it

We use citizen information solely to attempt to identify a volunteer lawyer who may be willing to consider representing you, and to communicate with you about that effort. We use lawyer information solely to evaluate and manage volunteer matches.

How it is stored and protected

  • The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Submitted information is intended to be stored in a secured, access-controlled system with encryption at rest, rather than in plain email.
  • Access is limited to authorized Clearinghouse staff and volunteers on a need-to-know basis.
  • Any uploaded files are restricted to permitted types (PDF), scanned for malware, and stored under the same access controls as other form data.

Who it may be shared with

If a potential match is identified, we may share basic case information (such as your name, contact information, the court, and the case number) with a prospective volunteer lawyer so that the lawyer can check for conflicts of interest and decide whether to contact you. We do not sell your information. We may disclose information if required by law.
